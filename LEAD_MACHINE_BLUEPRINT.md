## 📋 THE SEQUENCE (What Actually Happens)

### Default 21-Day Sequence:

| Day | Channel | Content | Goal |
|-----|---------|---------|------|
| 1 | EMAIL | Introduction + value prop | Open door |
| 2 | SMS | Short personal touch | Get attention |
| 4 | EMAIL | Case study / proof | Build trust |
| 7 | SMS | Quick question | Engage |
| 10 | EMAIL | More value + soft ask | Move forward |
| 14 | SMS | "Worth a quick chat?" | Direct ask |
| 17 | EMAIL | Final value bomb | Last push |
| 21 | EMAIL | Breakup email | Create urgency |

### Response Handling:

**If they REPLY:**
- Pause sequence immediately
- AI classifies intent
- Route appropriately:
  - "Interested" → Send Calendly link
  - "Questions" → Answer, continue conversation
  - "Not now" → Schedule follow-up in 30 days
  - "Unsubscribe" → Remove, mark do-not-contact

**If they BOOK:**
- Mark as "Meeting Scheduled"
- Send confirmation
- Create deal in pipeline
- Prep meeting notes

**If NO response after 21 days:**
- Move to "Long-term nurture"
- Monthly value emails only
- Re-engage in 90 days
