# The QUALITY TRIANGLE - Your Ideal Prospect Filter

## 🔺 The Three Equal Sides (All Required)

### **Side 1: TIME TO INVEST** ⏰
**What This Means:**
- Has hours per week to dedicate to building
- Not looking for "passive" or "push-button" income
- Understands this is REAL WORK
- Willing to learn and implement systems
- Can commit to consistent action

**Red Flags (Exclude):**
- "I'm too busy to do anything"
- "Can this run on autopilot?"
- "How little time does this take?"
- "I just want passive income"

**Green Flags (Include):**
- "I can dedicate 10-20 hours/week"
- "Willing to learn and implement"
- "Looking to build something real"
- "Understand it takes work"

---

### **Side 2: FINANCIAL STABILITY** 💰
**What This Means:**
- Can afford food, shelter, clothing, transportation
- NOT desperately broke
- Has some buffer (not living paycheck to paycheck crisis)
- Can invest time without starving
- Not in financial emergency mode

**Red Flags (Exclude):**
- "I need money RIGHT NOW"
- "Can't pay rent next month"
- "In foreclosure/eviction"
- "Credit is destroyed"
- "Bankruptcy filing"
- "Desperate for income"
- Broke-ass motherfuckers (your words! 😄)

**Green Flags (Include):**
- "Have my basics covered"
- "Looking to add income stream"
- "Can invest in myself"
- "Have severance/savings buffer"
- "Not desperate, but motivated"

---

### **Side 3: INTACT CREDIBILITY** ✅
**What This Means:**
- Haven't destroyed reputation with scammy MLMs
- Haven't burned bridges with family/friends
- Haven't promoted pyramid schemes
- Don't have history of overhyping/misleading
- Personal integrity intact
- People still trust them

**Red Flags (Exclude):**
- Serial MLM hopper (join and quit constantly)
- Known for promoting scams
- "Rent a Lambo" fake wealth posts
- Overhypes everything
- Burned bridges with opportunity-pushing
- Involved in lawsuits/fraud
- "Fake it til you make it" history
- Deceptive marketing tactics

**Green Flags (Include):**
- Professional reputation intact
- Family/friends still talk to them
- Haven't alienated their network
- Known for integrity
- May have tried MLM but didn't destroy credibility
- Open about past experiences honestly
- "I used to hate MLM until I found the right one"

## 🎯 Qualification Questions (Pre-Screen)

### **Before You Waste Time, Ask:**

**Q1: "Do you have 10-15 hours per week to dedicate to building?"**
- If "no" → Not qualified (no time)
- If "is it passive?" → Not qualified (wrong mindset)

**Q2: "Are you financially stable with basics covered?"**
- If "desperate for money" → Not qualified (broke)
- If "need money THIS WEEK" → Not qualified (wrong energy)

**Q3: "Have you been involved in MLM before?"**
- If "yes, dozens of times" → CAUTION (serial hopper?)
- If "yes, burned all my bridges" → Not qualified (credibility gone)
- If "no" or "yes, once, didn't work out" → Could be good

**Q4: "What's your professional background?"**
- Listen for credibility signals
- Corporate = usually good
- "Between scams" = NOT good
